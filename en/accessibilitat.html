Accessibility commitment

Associació Educació i Lleure Ubuntu is committed to making its website accessible, in accordance with Royal Decree 1112/2018, of 7 September, on the accessibility of public sector websites and mobile applications, and with standard UNE-EN 301549 v3.2.1 (equivalent to WCAG 2.1 level AA).

This website's accessibility tools

At the top of each page you'll find the “Accessibility” bar with: Dark / Light (dark mode with moon icon and light mode with sun icon), A− / A+ (font size, between 12 and 24 px) and Readable font (font and spacing that make reading easier). Your preferences are saved in your browser and kept as you browse the site. In addition, all images include descriptive alternative text (alt).

Compliance status

This website is partially compliant with Royal Decree 1112/2018 due to exceptions and non-conformity in certain aspects detailed below.

Non-accessible content

Non-compliance with the standard

  • Some interactive elements may not be fully keyboard-accessible.
  • Some embedded videos (YouTube) may lack subtitles or audio description.
  • Some older images may lack suitable alternative text.
  • Colour contrast in some sections may not meet the required minimum (4.5:1).

Disproportionate burden

Not applicable.

Content outside the scope of the applicable legislation

Third-party content (YouTube videos, maps, etc.) not developed by Associació Ubuntu.

How this statement was prepared

This statement was prepared on 6 October 2026.

This statement was prepared following a self-assessment by the Associació's technical team.

Feedback and contact details

You can report any requirement under clause 9.2 of Royal Decree 1112/2018 (accessible information about content outside the scope, or exempt from accessibility requirements for imposing a disproportionate burden) via ubuntu@associacioubuntu.org.

Enforcement procedure

If, after submitting the communication, it has been rejected, or the response is unsatisfactory, the interested party may lodge a complaint with the competent accessibility body.
