CAU

Educational leisure space for children aged 6 to 12, on Saturday afternoons.

Home / Projects / CAU

Who are we?

Ubuntu's cau is an educational leisure space where children and families take part in activities built on togetherness, care and guidance.

What do we do?

This space runs a leisure activity offering children a place to play and learn, building their self-esteem, skills, knowledge and values.

When?

The activity takes place on Saturdays from 4 pm to 6:30 pm

Where?

At Escola Ferrer i Guàrdia. C/Pedraforca, 3.

Who is it for?

For local children aged 6 to 12.
