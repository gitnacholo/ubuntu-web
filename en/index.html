About us

An association born in the neighbourhood

In 2008 we started out as a Saturday-afternoon leisure space in Zona Nord, Ciutat Meridiana. Eight years later, seeing neighbourhood families' needs, Associació Educació i Lleure Ubuntu was born: “I am because you are”.

We are a team of dedicated professionals and volunteers. Thanks to the community, Ubuntu grows year after year and reaches more children and families.
