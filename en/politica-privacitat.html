Privacy policy

This notice informs you that any personal data provided through this website will be processed by Associació Educació i Lleure Ubuntu, C/ Pedraforca, 3, 08033, ubuntu@associacioubuntu.org (hereinafter, the Service Provider).

Purpose of processing

We process data you share solely to handle your request or query made through this website and, where you expressly consent by ticking the box at the bottom of the data collection form, to send you information about the Service Provider's activities that may interest you, by any means including electronic ones.

Legal basis

Consent, which is the legal basis legitimising data processing, may be withdrawn at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent given before its withdrawal.

Required data

Unless specifically stated otherwise, all required form fields must be completed. If any required data is missing, the Service Provider may decline to process the request.

Data retention

Data provided will be kept until users decide to withdraw consent to processing.

Data accuracy

Users must complete forms with true, accurate, complete and up-to-date data, and are liable for any damage caused by defective forms completed with false, inaccurate, incomplete or outdated data.

Security

The Service Provider has adopted the legally required levels of personal data protection, putting in place the technical and organisational measures needed to prevent loss, misuse, alteration, unauthorised access and other possible risks.

The Service Provider likewise upholds its duty of confidentiality over personal data and will take all measures needed to prevent alteration, loss, processing or unauthorised access, keeping the state of technology in mind at all times.

User rights

Data subjects have the right to request access, rectification or erasure, restriction of processing, objection and portability, as set out in the General Data Protection Regulation, by writing to Associació Educació i Lleure Ubuntu at c/ Pedraforca, 3, and/or ubuntu@associacioubuntu.org, proving the user's identity.

Amendments

The Service Provider may amend this policy, informing users of any changes beforehand.

Complaints

Users may complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency if they believe their data protection rights have been infringed.
