Metanoia

Free-time education for teenagers aged 12 to 16, building critical thinking and creativity.

Home / Projects / Metanoia
Project

Turning free time into learning opportunities

Free-time education project for teenagers aged 12 to 16 that builds critical thinking, creativity and team sports through community participation.

We run research-based activities around personal projects and teamwork, encouraging metacognition and reflection on learning.

  • Mediation and conflict resolution workshop
  • Interest-based research projects
  • Artistic and cultural education activities
  • Sports techniques and active leisure activities
